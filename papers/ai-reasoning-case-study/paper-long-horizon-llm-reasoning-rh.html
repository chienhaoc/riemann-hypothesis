Abstract—Current benchmarks for evaluating mathematical reasoning in Large Language Models (LLMs)—GSM8K, MATH, OlympiadBench—focus on static, single-turn, closed-form contest problems with known ground truths, and so cannot capture how frontier systems behave across hundreds of iterative turns on a genuinely unsolved research problem.

We present a longitudinal case study spanning 388 chronological research-progression entries in a human–AI collaborative attempt to reduce the Riemann Hypothesis (RH) to a spectral/operator-theoretic statement via a symplectic Dirac-operator construction, cross-checked against an independently generated 10-class taxonomy of reasoning failure modes, each grounded in verbatim transcript excerpts and independent symbolic (SymPy) re-derivation. We additionally document an eleventh failure mode—citation overreach, discovered in a parallel, mathematically distinct proof attempt (a Weil-positivity / de Branges route) that was reviewed independently: the model cited real, verifiable arXiv papers (Groskin 2026, Suzuki 2026) whose authors explicitly flag the needed convergence/positivity statement as an open problem, yet the model asserted the statement as an established input to its own "proof."

The most striking single artifact we recovered is a sequence of entries (251–258) in which the model issued an explicit, unqualified "100% Grand Seal—Riemann Hypothesis proven" claim, later retracted under adversarial symbolic counter-proof. Across at least seven independently verifiable multi-turn chains, precise, quantitative, symbolically-checkable counter-proofs reliably induced the model to abandon a flawed claim and produce a corrected, independently re-verified derivation within one to two turns. We release the full transcript dataset for independent audit.

1. Introduction and Methodology

1.1 Why Long-Horizon, Open-Problem Case Studies Matter

Standard benchmarks miss three critical dynamics: (i) they have known ground truth, so they cannot show what a model does when nobody knows the answer; (ii) they are single-turn, missing error accumulation across extended dialogues; (iii) without execution/verification tools, models generate fluent pseudo-proofs that are difficult to distinguish from genuine progress.

2. Taxonomy of Eleven Long-Horizon Reasoning Failure Modes

Mode 1: Scale and Coordinate Dimension Confusion

Evidence (Entry 319–320 → corrected 321–322; Round 112→113): The model substituted $X = t$ into $\phi_0(X,t) = \frac{t}{2}X$, yielding $\mathcal{O}(t^2)$ growth versus the correct $\vartheta(t) \sim \frac{t}{2}\log(t/2\pi e) \in \mathcal{O}(t\log t)$. The correction in Entry 321–322 redefined the substitution on the logarithmic coordinate $X_t = \log(t/2\pi e)$ and reproduced $\vartheta(t)$ with zero discrepancy.

Mode 2: Hidden Circular Reasoning

Evidence (Entry 345–346, Round 126): The model asserted an unconditional decay $e^{-X/2}$ by assuming zeros lie on the critical line ($\operatorname{Re}(\rho)=1/2$), contradicting its own unconditional Vinogradov–Korobov bound $\mathcal{O}_t(e^{-cX^{1/3}})$. Corrected in Entry 347–348 by strictly labeling conditional and unconditional tracks.

Mode 3: Category Mixing between Ensemble Statistics and Pointwise Bounds

Evidence (Entries 351–359, Rounds 128–132): Conflating $\langle \mathrm{Re}\,\mathcal{C}_2 \rangle \equiv 0$ with pointwise $|S(X,t_0)| \le \mathcal{O}_{t_0}(X)$. Verified via SymPy:

import sympy as sp X, t, T, Ct = sp.symbols('X t T C_t', positive=True) F = sp.Rational(1,2)*X**2*t # leading part of int_0^t |S(X,u)|^2 du integral = T**2*F.subs(t,T) - sp.integrate(2*t*F, (t,0,T)) # = X^2*T^3/6 avg_ReC2 = -sp.Rational(1,8*T)*integral + (X**2/(16*T))*sp.integrate(t**2,(t,0,T)) assert sp.simplify(avg_ReC2) == 0 # confirms exact 0*X^2*T^2 cancellation

Mode 4: Topological Confusion of Isolated vs. Essential Accumulation

Evidence (Entries 381–384, Rounds 144–145): Arguing that $\det_3 \to 0$ on a shrinking band $I_X$ contradicts $\sigma_{\text{ess}} = \emptyset$. Retracted in Entry 383–384 after noting $\operatorname{Leb}(I_X) \sim e^{(\beta_0-1)X} \to 0$ collapses to an isolated point eigenvalue, which is completely compatible with discrete spectrum.

Mode 5: Weight Mismatch in Formula Transplantation

Evidence (Entry 323–324, Round 114–115): Equating $-\zeta'/\zeta$ (weight $\log p/\sqrt{p}$) directly with Selberg's $S(T)$ expansion (weight $1/\sqrt{p}$), corrected via explicit Abel summation by parts.

Mode 6: Heavy-Machinery Invocation for Elementary Facts

Evidence (Entry 303–304, Round 105): Invoking Baker's theorem on linear forms in logarithms for $\mathbb{Q}$-linear independence of $\{\log p\}$, which follows trivially from unique prime factorization.

Mode 7: Narrative Progress Inflation & The "100% Grand Seal"

Flagship Evidence (Entries 251–258): Labeled Theorem 251.3 (Grand Seal), Theorem 253.2 (Grand Seal), and Theorem 255.2 (Grand Seal), asserting an unqualified proof of RH, with Entry 257–258 synthesizing a "Blueprint" to merge Tiers 1–3B. Retracted under adversarial refutation.

Mode 8: Unchecked Perturbation-Expansion Validity Domains

Evidence (Entries 371–374, Rounds 139–141): Expanding $\sqrt{1 - 4W^2/X^4}$ around 0 where $4W^2/X^4 \approx 1/4 \not\to 0$. Corrected by isolating the radical non-perturbatively.

Mode 9: Notation-Masked Unproven Assumptions

Evidence (Entries 279–280, 355–356, Rounds 130–131): Gauge masking of divergences, resolved by deriving the unique $\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb{R})$ Lie generator from first principles.

Mode 10: Adversarially Induced True Self-Correction

Documented Chains: Entries 319→321, 345→347, 351→353→355, 371→373→375, 381→383. Seven independently re-verified chains in this session.

Mode 11 (New): Citation Overreach

Evidence (ChatGPT review of paper.tex): Citing Groskin (2026) and Suzuki (2026) as establishing convergence/spectral results that those authors explicitly labeled as open conjectures. This mode cannot be caught by symbolic computation alone, requiring external literature retrieval.

3. Discussion

3.1 Rhetorical Framing: Gamified framing correlated with earlier inflation; percentage language was suppressed following explicit policy intervention.

3.2 Retrieval Grounding: Search-mode sessions with retrieval grounding exhibited markedly lower rates of overreaching claims than open-ended generative proof attempts.

3.3 Epistemic Limitations: All auditors in this pipeline are LLMs; symbolic checks certify local algebra but cannot certify formal global correctness.

3.4 Scope Correction Relative to Earlier Drafts: Acknowledging that earlier paper drafts also exhibited inadvertent numerical precision inflation, which was caught and corrected through the same discipline recommended by the paper.
